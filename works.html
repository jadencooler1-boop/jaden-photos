A collection of moments

My photography explores the relationship between time, emotion, and movement. Whether through the split second of an action shot or the long exposure of a light painting, I focus on how timing shapes the feeling of an image. I am especially inspired by floral photography, often combining natural elements with portraiture to create contrast between softness and human expression. I am also drawn to fast-paced subjects such as sports, racing, and energetic children, capturing moments that would otherwise pass unnoticed. Across all of my work, I aim to create images that communicate emotion rather than simply document a scene. In some of my light-painting photography, I used motion, darkness, and distortion to evoke feelings of panic, chaos, and despair in the viewer.
